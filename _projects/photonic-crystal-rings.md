## Overview

Ring resonators trap light in a tight loop, which makes them ideal for nonlinear optics, but
what they can do depends heavily on their **dispersion**: how the resonance frequencies are
spaced. A photonic crystal ring adds a periodic modulation around the ring, which couples
forward- and backward-traveling light and shifts chosen resonances. That gives a designer a
precise knob for engineering the dispersion. I designed a set of these rings and had them
fabricated through Nokia.
