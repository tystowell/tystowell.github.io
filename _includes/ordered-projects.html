{%- comment -%} Builds two lists: - `chrono_projects`: every project, newest first (the /work/ grid and Newer/Older links). - `ordered_projects`: grouped by category (in the order of _data/categories.yml), newest first within each category (the work index and "Jump to…" sidebar). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign chrono_projects = site.projects | sort: "date" | reverse -%} {%- assign ordered_projects = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for cat in site.data.categories -%} {%- assign in_cat = chrono_projects | where: "category", cat.id -%} {%- assign ordered_projects = ordered_projects | concat: in_cat -%} {%- endfor -%}
