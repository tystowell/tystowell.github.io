My name is Tyler Stowell, and I'm a research assistant in Dr. Camacho's lab at Brigham Young University researching quantum engineering. I made this website for a couple of reasons, but the main one is that I think resume's and CV's are boring. I've done a lot of cool things in my life, and I'm proud of what I've been able to accomplish. I wanted to share some of that in a format that wasn't quite so formal, and I wanted a good record of the path I've taken to end up where I am.

If I had to define myself, I'd say that I'm curious and determined. I have a mix of always wanting to try new things and having the drive to see them through. I taught myself to code, I taught myself physics, I taught myself robotics and I taught myself quantum mechanics. Frequently I find something cool and ask myself, "how can I learn to do that?" That's the source of most of the projects on this website.

I also hope that my voice comes through in some of these writeups. Working on this website has reminded me just how fortunate I've been to have the opportunities I do, and I'm so grateful to the people who made it possible. Some of these projects worked, some of these projects didn't, some of them led to publications and some of them led to a Github repository that's now gathering dust. I'm grateful for all of them, and I hope that I can continue working on new things that push the limits of what I'm capable of.

Enjoy learning a little more about me, and I hope you find this page as cool as I do! (Note: if you only have time to skim, go check out [my work page]({{ '/work/' | relative_url }}) - it has the best pictures)
